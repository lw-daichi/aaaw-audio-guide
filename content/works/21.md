翼を大きく横に広げたフクロウを、彫り出した作品。アイヌの人々のあいだで、シマフクロウは村を守る神コタンコㇿカムイとして敬われてきました。阿寒湖アイヌコタンでは、フクロウやクマ、シャチといった自然界に宿るカムイの姿が、木彫の題材として彫り継がれてきました。大きく広げた翼のかたちには、木と向き合い、刃を入れ続けた作家の時間が刻まれています。

===

A carved work of an owl with its wings spread wide to the sides. Among the Ainu people, the Blakiston's fish owl has been revered as Kotankor Kamuy, the god who protects the village. At the Akanko Ainu Kotan, the forms of kamuy dwelling in the natural world—owls, bears, orcas—have been carved and handed down as subjects of woodcarving. In the shape of the widely spread wings is inscribed the time of an artist who faced the wood and kept setting his blade to it.
