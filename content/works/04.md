アイヌ古式舞踊の踊り手にも携わる阿寒湖在住の宮川ヌエを主な対象とした、交流と対話の軌跡を残し表現した作品。
物理的に顔を合わせたことのない相手に向けたドローイングと文字の日記交換から始まった二人の対話は、阿寒で初めて顔を合わせながら行われました。
コミュニケーションの痕跡は大きな布に描かれた線と、交わした手紙から辿ることができます。それぞれの痕跡を互いになぞることで言葉にしきれない感覚を交換しながら、お互いに関係し合う。

===

A work that records and gives form to the trail of exchange and dialogue, centered mainly on Nue Miyakawa, a resident of Lake Akan who is also a dancer of traditional Ainu dance. The two artists' dialogue began as an exchange of drawings and written diaries addressed to someone they had never met in person, and was then carried on when they met for the first time in Akan. The traces of their communication can be followed in the lines drawn on a large cloth and in the letters they exchanged. By tracing each other's marks, they exchange sensations that words cannot fully capture, and come to relate to one another.
