丁寧に彫り上げられたアイヌ伝統の小刀「マキリ」。アイヌ紋様の大胆なパターンの中の
細やかな意匠は、アイヌの木彫りの伝統技術である「うろこ彫り」。
非常に細やかで丁寧なその彫り跡に、彼の技術の確かさが見て取れます。

===

A makiri—the traditional Ainu small knife—carved with great care. Within the bold Ainu patterns, the fine detailing is "uroko-bori" (scale carving), a traditional technique of Ainu woodcarving. The sureness of his skill can be seen in those extremely fine, meticulous carving marks.
