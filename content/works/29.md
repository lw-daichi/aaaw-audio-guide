===

For lunch, the woman I met in Akan (82) ate shoyu ramen, American crayfish, takoyaki, vinegared mushrooms, ice cream, grapes, and a corn dog, and said, "Because living is survival." —And so, a survival strategy in a harsh natural environment and the gluttony of the present strangely intersect. All the while, she talked about "how to eat the scallops for dinner."
