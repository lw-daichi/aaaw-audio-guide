阿寒に住む人々やその手元、日常の情景を描いたドローイング作品。作者が日々の生活から心を揺さぶられる瞬間を切り取り、丁寧に表現されている。日常の何気ない一瞬が独自の視点で描かれ、地域に根ざした温かみと力強さが感じられる作品。

===

A series of drawings depicting the people of Akan, their hands at work, and scenes of everyday life. The artist captures the moments from daily life that move her and renders them with care. Casual, fleeting instants of the everyday are drawn through her own distinctive eye, giving the work a warmth and strength rooted in the community.
