アイヌの男性が儀式の時などの正装用に身につける「エムㇱアッ(刀を下げる帯)」。アイヌの女性が夫や息子のために織り上げてきました。細い糸で文様を織り出す技法は、ブレスレットという現代の生活の中に馴染んでゆきます。

===

The emush-at (a sash for carrying a sword) is worn by Ainu men as formal attire for ceremonies and the like. Ainu women have woven them for their husbands and sons. The technique of weaving patterns from fine thread finds its place within the bracelet, an object of contemporary life.
