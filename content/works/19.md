アイヌ民族に古くから伝わる刀。
狩猟や漁で使用され、小型の短刀を「マキリ」、マキリより大型の山刀を「タシロ」と呼ぶ。木製の鞘や柄には精緻なアイヌ文様が彫刻され、日常の道具にも細やかに美を宿すアイヌ民族の美意識が表れる伝統的彫刻作品。

===

Blades handed down among the Ainu people since ancient times. Used for hunting and fishing, the small short blade is called makiri, and the larger mountain knife, larger than the makiri, is called tasiro. Intricate Ainu patterns are carved into the wooden sheath and handle—a traditional carved work in which the Ainu aesthetic, lending delicate beauty even to everyday tools, comes to the fore.
