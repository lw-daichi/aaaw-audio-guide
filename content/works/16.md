木綿の布地に直接刺繍を施して文様を描き出す衣服で、ここ阿寒ではチンジリと呼ばれています。布を重ねず、糸の線だけで文様を表すため、つくり手の手の運びがそのまま現れます。本作を手がけた西田香代子は、阿寒のフチたちから刺繍を学び、丁寧で柔らかな線の一本一本に、長く針を重ねてきた時間が宿っています。

===

A garment whose patterns are drawn by embroidering directly onto cotton fabric, called chinjiri here in Akan. Because no cloth is layered and the patterns are expressed by the lines of thread alone, the movement of the maker's hand appears just as it is. Kayoko Nishida, who made this work, learned embroidery from the fuchi (elder women) of Akan; in each careful, gentle line dwells the time of long hours spent with the needle.
